VellumCore Security Architecture & Zero-Retention Whitepaper

1. ZERO-DATA RETENTION (ZDR) PROTOCOL:
Under our SOC2 Type II controls, all documents transmitted for Claude 5.5 Sonnet inference reside exclusively in volatile, ephemeral RAM. Immediately upon webhook delivery or client receipt, raw buffers and vector projections are purged with zero persistent disk write.
2. ENCRYPTION AT REST & IN TRANSIT:
All network ingress is bound to TLS 1.3 with AES-256-GCM cipher suites. Webhook deliveries are authenticated with SHA-256 HMAC cryptographic signatures.
3. ANTHROPIC CLAUDE COMMERCIAL AGREEMENT:
Inference workloads operate under Anthropic’s Zero Data Retention commercial terms. Client payloads are explicitly excluded from foundation model training.
4. ACADEMIC & INSTITUTIONAL AUDIT:
Institutional research groups (.edu / .edu.tr) may request dedicated audit logs and VPC peering verification by contacting security@vellumcore.ai.
